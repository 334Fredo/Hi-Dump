# FLAC to WAV & MP3 Converter

A simple, free, and transparent audio converter designed for DJs who want to preserve the way they organize their music outside of DJ software.

The idea is simple: start with a rich, high-quality source and convert it into the format you actually need, while making as few compromises as possible.

The converter supports **FLAC → WAV** conversion for lossless, bit-perfect preservation, as well as **MP3 conversion at different sampling rates and quality levels**.

What makes it particularly useful for DJ libraries is that it **preserves the original folder structure**. Your files are converted while keeping the same organization as the source library, so the way you have carefully arranged your music outside of software such as Serato or Rekordbox remains intact.

Think of it as a funnel:

**Rich source material → appropriate format → minimal compromise**

Rather than reorganizing, managing, or locking your music into a specific DJ ecosystem, the goal is simply to transform the files while respecting the structure you created yourself.

### Why?

DJ libraries can become complex over time. Folders, subfolders, genres, labels, sets, eras, personal classifications — this organization often exists independently from Serato, Rekordbox, or any other software.

This tool is made to respect that work.

It is intentionally **simple, fluid, and free**, with no unnecessary features or proprietary ecosystem. You provide the source folder, choose the output format and quality, and the converter takes care of the rest.

### Transparency

The project is openly available on GitHub because transparency matters.

There is no hidden conversion process or black box: the code is available for anyone who wants to inspect, understand, or contribute to it.

The goal is not to reinvent audio conversion, but to make a practical workflow for DJs that is straightforward, reliable, and respectful of their existing music organization.
